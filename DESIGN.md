DESIGN.md — Yalçın Pastaneleri

1. Design Philosophy

Create a premium, elegant and authentic digital experience for Yalçın Pastaneleri.

The website and React Native application must feel like products designed by a professional design studio, not generated by AI.

Core principles:

* Apple-level simplicity and precision.
* Premium bakery and patisserie aesthetics.
* Authentic Turkish brand identity.
* Minimal, elegant and timeless interfaces.
* Strong typography, generous whitespace.
* Real photography over decorative graphics.
* Consistent design across web, iOS and Android.

2. Visual Identity

Use Yalçın Pastaneleri’s actual logo, brand colors and visual identity.

Never invent a new logo or replace existing brand elements.

Color system:

* Background: #FFFFFF
* Secondary background: #FAF9F6
* Primary text: #1C1C1C
* Secondary text: #737373
* Borders: #EAEAEA
* Accent: Derived from the actual brand identity.

Avoid excessive gradients, artificial shadows, neon colors and unnecessary decorations.

3. Typography

Use premium, highly legible typography.

* Website: Inter / Geist
* Mobile: SF Pro (iOS), Roboto (Android)
* Clear hierarchy and balanced spacing.
* Large, elegant headlines.
* Consistent font weights.
* No excessive bold text or decorative fonts.

4. Layout & Components

* Clean, structured grid layouts.
* Mobile-first responsive design.
* Consistent 8px spacing system.
* Subtle corner radii: 8–16px.
* Large, high-quality product photography.
* Minimal navigation.
* Elegant product cards.
* Clear pricing and availability.
* Accessible buttons and forms.
* Native mobile interactions.

Avoid generic dashboard layouts and repetitive AI-generated card designs.

5. Photography & Assets

Only authentic Yalçın Pastaneleri assets are permitted.

* Real product photography.
* Actual bakery and branch photographs.
* Original brand logo.
* Authentic Instagram content with appropriate permission.
* No AI-generated food photography.
* No stock bakery images.
* No fake customer testimonials.
* Never distort, regenerate or artificially alter products.

Preserve natural lighting, realistic textures and accurate product colors.

6. Website Experience

The website must be visually refined, fast and conversion-focused.

Primary pages:

* Home
* Menu / Products
* Product Details
* Custom Cake Orders
* Cart / Checkout
* Branches
* About Us
* Contact

Use authentic photography, editorial-style sections and subtle transitions.

SEO, responsive behavior and performance are mandatory.

7. Mobile Experience

Build a polished, native-feeling React Native application.

Primary navigation:

* Home
* Explore / Menu
* Orders
* Favorites
* Profile

Requirements:

* Native navigation patterns.
* Smooth scrolling.
* Bottom tab navigation.
* Clear product customization.
* Simple checkout.
* Order tracking.
* Push notification support.
* Proper loading, empty and error states.

Avoid web-like mobile interfaces.

8. Motion & Interactions

Animations must be purposeful and restrained.

* Duration: 150–300ms.
* Natural easing.
* Subtle press feedback.
* Smooth screen transitions.
* Skeleton loading where appropriate.
* Respect reduced-motion settings.

No excessive parallax, bouncing elements or decorative animations.

9. Strict Anti-AI-Slop Rules

Absolutely prohibited:

* Generic AI-generated website aesthetics.
* Fake product photography.
* Random gradients and glowing effects.
* Excessive rounded cards.
* Emoji-based interface elements.
* Decorative illustrations without purpose.
* Fake statistics or testimonials.
* Placeholder marketing content in production.
* Inconsistent spacing and typography.
* Unnecessary visual complexity.

Every component must serve a clear user purpose.

10. Quality Standard

The final experience must be:

Authentic. Premium. Minimal. Fast. Consistent. Production-ready.

Before implementing any interface, verify consistency with this document, AGENTS.md and assets.md.

Never sacrifice usability for visual decoration.
